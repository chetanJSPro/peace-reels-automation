## Style breakdown

### Visuals

- Mostly vertical travel/nature/spiritual Indian scenery.
- Places/themes visible from titles: Rishikesh, Ganga, Kashi/Varanasi, Girnar, Haridwar, Himachal/nature.
- Common emotional combination: calm scenic shot + short moral/spiritual text.

### Text overlays

- A small **top location label** often works like a location/pin sticker.
- Bottom/center captions are large, high contrast, and readable.
- Text often uses Hinglish/Roman Hindi rather than pure formal Hindi.
- Emojis are common: 🕊 📿 💯 ❤️ 😇 🍂.

### Voice/music

- The user observed human-type AI voice and subtitles.
- For a legitimate clone-resistant system, use your own original script + a commercial-friendly local TTS voice. Do **not** imitate a living person's voice without permission.
- Background music should be from a source that allows commercial YouTube use, and the track/source should be stored in the rights log.

### Title/hashtag formula

Common structure:

`short emotional phrase + emoji + #meditation #innerpeace #rishikesh/#ganga/#kashi #travel #explore`

Do not blindly copy their exact hashtags such as other creator names or irrelevant trending tags. Irrelevant tags can harm trust and may be treated as spammy metadata.

## The “pinpoint top text” solution

Use a top label template:

- `📍 RISHIKESH | UTTARAKHAND`
- `📍 GANGA GHAT | INDIA`
- `📍 KASHI | VARANASI`
- `📍 HIMALAYAN MORNING`
- `📍 NATURE | INNER PEACE`

Important legitimacy rule: **never label generic stock footage as a specific place unless the source confirms it.** If a Pexels clip is only “river in India,” use a generic label like `GANGA GHAT | INDIA` or `NATURE | INNER PEACE`.

## Content strategy based on research

### Primary format: Shorts

Create 20–45 second Shorts with:

1. Hook: 1 emotional sentence.
2. 4–5 short reflective lines.
3. Calm scenic vertical footage.
4. Human-sounding open TTS voice.
5. Big captions burned in.
6. Top location/pin label.
7. Soft background music.
8. A simple CTA: “Save this for peace” or “Follow for daily inner peace.”

### Secondary format: weekly long video

Once per week, make an 8–15 minute guided breathing/meditation video. Keep it more original and useful than the reference channel's generic long videos:

- Add structure: intro, method, safety note, practice, reflection.
- Avoid false claims such as guaranteed third-eye opening, disease cures, or miracles.
- Use calm original narration and cite any source/tradition when relevant.

## Monetization direction

Do not rely only on Shorts ad revenue. Use Shorts as discovery and build a funnel:

1. Shorts bring subscribers.
2. Long videos build watch time and trust.
3. Descriptions link to:
   - Free meditation PDF/checklist.
   - Email/WhatsApp community.
   - Affiliate products with disclosure: books, yoga mat, mala, meditation cushion, travel essentials.
   - Your own digital product: guided meditation pack, Rishikesh peaceful itinerary, journaling template.

## Legal/originality rules

- Do not download or reuse the channel's videos/audio.
- Do not clone the channel's voice.
- Do not copy scripts or exact captions.
- Use only your own footage or licensed/free stock footage.
- Keep source credits in every generated metadata file.
- Review each generated video before upload. YouTube monetization requires original/authentic value; mass-produced repetitive videos are risky.
